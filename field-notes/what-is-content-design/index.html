What is content design?

You will find 101 articles and definitions online all hopefully saying a similar thing, here is my take.

In short

The goal is to give the users all the information they need, when they need it, so they can complete the task at hand. Without it you might find them getting confused when using your product or not quite converting in the way you'd imagine.

In slightly longer

Content design was coined by Sarah Winters as she found that the term writing didn't quite reflect the level of effort going into the content work she was doing.

Research, user needs, journey mapping, language, creation, sharing, and iteration, the content is being designed, not just written.

The discipline is about taking a step back from the output, and refocusing on the thinking beforehand, pairing an understanding of the audience, with an understanding of the business. A content designer (ideally working collaboratively) can create a solution that communicates exactly what is needed so that both parties get the win.

Most of the time the content will be words designed and written in a way that is easy to understand in the audience's context, but not always. If the user needs call for it, the best way to communicate may be cutting words from a page, or creating a feature like a calculator, or an asset like an infographic or video.

The guiding principle is the user always comes first.

What's in a name?

It is not lost on the content design community, a community focused on being clear and concise, that there are multiple roles and titles that pretty much mean the same thing. Content designer, UX writer, content strategy to name but a few. Some folk will die on the hill that they're all separate things, I don't disagree, but I don't think it is worth getting bogged down in. The roles and responsibilities along with the approach and the goal are ultimately the same.

A soft sell

The buried lede in all of this, content design is an essential role in delivering the user experience that your audience needs. If you haven't already, you should look into it.
