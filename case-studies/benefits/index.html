Giving employees what they need to choose

The impact

6.4 / 7How easy employees found choosing, up from 5.7
Only 3%Of sessions ended in a support enquiry

The situation

Benivo's employee platform gives relocating employees access to their policy based relocation services and benefits, like home search, shipping and visa support. However, employees were not initiating their benefits or were confused about what they had available to them.

Some visited the benefits page over fifty times before committing. This meant clients kept fielding the same questions, what do I have, how much can I spend, what happens if I choose this, despite a Benivo feature built to do all this.

Action taken

The brief was to fix a confusing flow. Discovery work showed the product wasn't supporting how people were actually behaving: researching and comparing before committing.

The flow and content was rebuilt around that behaviour. Categories, transparent pricing and a persistent budget display replaced hidden costs and buried menu actions. Each service's detail page was designed content-first, giving people what they needed to decide:

  • What the service is and who provides it.
  • What other employees thought.
  • What it costs against their remaining budget.
  • What's needed to act.

Employees whose employer hadn't configured full benefits now got a route into Benivo's own partner network, instead of an empty page.

The result

The ease-of-selection score rose from 5.7 to 6.4 out of 7 after launch. Support enquiries settled at around 3% of sessions, a strong sign the product itself was doing the explaining, not the support team.
