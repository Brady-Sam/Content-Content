AI & Chatbot · B2B SaaS

Turning an AI chatbot into a trusted support channel

The impact

93%Questions answered without a support ticket
10 hoursSaved per week in the support team

The situation

A B2B SaaS platform connected an AI model to their support chatbot to triage and answer user questions. However, no content strategy or planning was included with the integration. Answers were generic, often wrong or hallucinated, including responses on client-specific policy, risking client escalations. Any time saved for the customer support team was spent cleaning up the errors.

Action taken

First the knowledge base the chatbot was pulling from was organised and redrafted into easy to understand (for both people and machines) answers. Then the guardrails and conversation design were shaped so that outputs were on brand, clear, and honest when it didn't have an answer. Collaborating with the development team meant these rules could catch when unanswered questions came in so they could be reviewed and the knowledge base could be updated.

The result

An increase from 69% to 93% of queries that were successfully answered by the AI. This change happened over a 6-month period, including the "peak season" of support requests. Users gained trust in the tool, and the support team got 10 hours' worth of time back in their week.
