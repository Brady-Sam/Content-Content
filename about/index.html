About

An origin story

10+ years working in tech, across product strategy, management and content design, I came to an unconfirmed conclusion:

Content design mainly appears across two broad industries, public services such as GOV.UK, and charities, and established SaaS companies.

There will be many exceptions to this rule, but the newer companies aren't getting the benefits of the discipline. It's something to look into later, or they survive without it.

Principle

Startups are built from a vision. Content Content's goal is to make sure that vision is communicated clearly throughout the product and service.

We're talking about practice

Why a practice?

Content Content has been built as a practice so the focus stays on solving client problems through the craft.

Scope

Startups are the focus, but not the limit. If the same problem shows up in scale-ups, charities, and larger SaaS teams, Content Content can and will help.

The human in the loop
