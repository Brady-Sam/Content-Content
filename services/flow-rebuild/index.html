The problem this solves

Take the findings of your Flow Review and put them into action. One journey fully rebuilt aligned to your business and audience needs.

What you get

  • Every screen in the named journey, rewritten to a finished standard, delivered where you need them, e.g. Figma, CMS, or repository
  • All states covered, not just the happy path, empty, loading, error, success, edge cases, and any others that don't exist but you need
  • A written rationale note explaining the decisions taken, for your team to refer back to
  • A handover naming anything I noticed outside the agreed journey worth your attention later
  • A short watchlist, two or three things to keep an eye on, what a good result looks like, and roughly when to expect it. A steer on what to watch to measure the impact of the changes

What's not included

This covers the one named journey, not your whole product. It doesn't include visual design, development or code implementation, ongoing content production, analytics setup, or translation, unless separately scoped. Additions to the journey during delivery count as new work, not an extension of this one.

How it works

  1. The priority journey is confirmed from your Flow Review findings.
  2. I issue your fixed price and delivery date.
  3. You accept, pay, and confirm your named contact and tool access.
  4. Delivery begins, starting with a required 60-minute kickoff call.
  5. I rebuild the journey end to end.
  6. An optional 30-minute check-in partway through, if you'd like one.
  7. Delivered in your tools, with one consolidated round of feedback, a 48-hour window, revisions capped at 2 hours.
  8. A 60-minute handover call, plus the rationale note and watchlist.
  9. Thirty days of support after handover for any questions that come up.

What I need from you

  • A completed Flow Review of this journey, this is how the fixed price stays honest, I won't quote a full rebuild on a journey I haven't properly looked at first
  • One named point of contact, able to respond within one working day during delivery
  • Working access to the environment I'll be delivering in

FAQs

Can I buy a Rebuild without doing a Flow Review first?

Not at a fixed price. I won't quote a fixed price on a journey I haven't properly looked at, that's what protects you from a guessed scope and protects me from underquoting the work. If you already know exactly what needs fixing and would rather skip that step, day rate work carries no such requirement, get in touch and I can talk you through that instead.

What if I want more changed once delivery's started?

Anything beyond the agreed journey is scoped and priced as new work, so your timeline and price stay reliable for what you signed up for.

What tools do you work in?

Whatever you already use, Figma, a CMS, or directly in your repository as a pull request for your engineers to review.
